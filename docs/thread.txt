Robinhood Chain has a Uniswap V2 pair and four V3 fee tiers live at once.

Five venues, same pair, different prices.

Most interfaces quote one and call it the price. I wanted to know what that costs.
---
3/
1 ETH → USDG, all five, same second:

V3 0.01%   2,486.66
V3 0.05%   2,484.43
V3 0.30%   2,479.76
V2 direct  2,471.48
V3 1.00%   2,340.95

The pool most routers default to is 0.61% worse. On 1 ETH that's $15 you handed over for nothing.

4/
So REFRACT quotes all six paths (V2 direct, V2 via WETH, four V3 tiers), ranks by output, and shows the shortfall on every route it didn't pick.

You can override it. The engine recommends. It doesn't hide the alternatives.

6/
Finding new tokens took three attempts.

I read the launch event wrong twice. topics[2] was a wallet, not a curve. topics[3] was a contract holding nothing.

What works: every launch mints full supply to the curve in one Transfer from 0x0. That mint IS the curve.
---
7/
That fix is better than what I was reaching for.

It doesn't depend on any launchpad's private ABI, so it keeps working when the next one ships a completely different event shape.

8/
Our rewards page once told a wallet it had routed 62,257,097 ETH.

It had routed 0.14.

I was reading the larger swap leg as the notional. Swap ETH for a memecoin with 1B supply and the memecoin side is enormous. I was calling that ETH.

Fixed: read the WETH side only.

10/
Public RPCs kept rate-limiting us. I throttled our requests. Helped a little.

Real cause: viem chunks multicalls by a 1024-byte default. Our batch of 460 calls was silently becoming ~30 requests.

I was rate-limiting one request that was quietly becoming thirty.
---
11/
Now when a scan does get limited, it's marked incomplete. The page shows a dash and says the RPC throttled us.

It never shows you a confident zero.

An empty result and a failed request look identical if you don't check. Almost nothing checks.
